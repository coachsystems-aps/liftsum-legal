Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my LiftSum Pro subscription?

Open LiftSum, navigate to Settings > LiftSum Pro, and tap Restore Purchases. Your subscription will sync instantly with your Apple ID.

Do I need an account to use LiftSum?

No. LiftSum requires zero sign-up or accounts. Your workouts, routines, and nutrition records live locally on your device for fast performance and maximum privacy.

How do I mark warm-up sets?

In any active workout, simply double-tap the set number numeral on the left of any set row. It will toggle to an amber W (Warm-Up). Warm-up sets are excluded from total volume and PR statistics.

How does the AI Meal Scanner work?

Tap the camera icon in the Nutrition tab, point at your plate, and tap the shutter. You can also tap the mic to dictate extra details (e.g., "extra virgin olive oil"). The AI analyzes your meal in seconds and presents itemized macros for your review.

Does LiftSum write to Apple Health?

No. LiftSum is strictly read-only with Apple Health. We read steps and active energy to calculate your net calorie burn above resting, and never overwrite your HealthKit logs.

How do I backup or export my data?

Go to Settings > Data & Storage. You can export a JSON archive of your entire workout history at any time. When you back up your iPhone to iCloud or your computer, your LiftSum data is automatically included.
