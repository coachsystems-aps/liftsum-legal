LiftSum

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026
Summary: LiftSum is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy. All your workout records, routines, nutrition logs, and personal metrics stay locally on your device. We do not require an account, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we never sell your data.

1. Local-First Data Storage

Your workouts, exercise history, custom routines, body weight, notes, personal records, and logged foods are stored directly in your iPhone's local encrypted storage (using Apple's SwiftData framework). You own your data. You can export or erase your data at any time inside the app under Settings > Data & Storage.

2. AI Meal Scanning & Camera Access

When you choose to use LiftSum's AI meal scanner, the app requests permission to access your device camera:

  • Barcode Scanning: Barcodes are scanned locally to look up food nutrition via the public Open Food Facts database.
  • AI Meal Recognition: When you snap a photo of a meal, that image (and your optional text or voice description) is transmitted securely over HTTPS to our backend proxy to identify foods and estimate nutritional information.
  • No Photo Storage: We do not store, retain, or train AI models on your meal photos. Once the nutritional estimation is returned to your device, the image data is discarded from server memory.
  • Anonymous Limit Enforcement: An anonymous device vendor identifier (identifierForVendor) is used strictly to enforce fair-use limits on daily AI scans.

3. Voice Dictation & Microphone Access

If you choose to use voice input to search for foods or dictate workout notes, LiftSum requests access to your microphone and Speech Recognition. Speech-to-text processing occurs on-device whenever supported by your iPhone (iOS 17+). Voice recordings are not stored or transmitted to third-party advertising networks.

4. Apple HealthKit Integration

LiftSum includes an optional, read-only integration with Apple HealthKit:

  • LiftSum reads daily step counts and active energy (calories burned) to calculate your net daily expenditure.
  • Read-Only: LiftSum never writes or modifies data inside your Apple Health store.
  • Strict Confidentiality: In accordance with Apple's Developer Guidelines, HealthKit data is never used for marketing, advertising, or shared with third parties. It remains strictly on your device to display your calorie budget.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All financial transactions and subscription renewals are managed directly by Apple via the App Store and StoreKit. LiftSum does not collect or have access to your credit card number, billing address, or payment details. We verify active subscription status using Apple's cryptographically signed receipts and store an anonymous transaction identifier only to track active membership seats.

6. Third-Party Services

LiftSum interacts with the following external services solely to provide core app functionality:

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: Payment processing and subscription management.
  • Open Food Facts: Public nutritional database for barcode and food searches.
  • Supabase: Secure cloud proxy used exclusively to manage AI scan requests and member quota verification.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because LiftSum does not operate user accounts on a remote server, all your application data lives on your device. You can immediately wipe all local data by deleting the app or using the "Reset Local Data" option in Settings.
